Driver & Fleet Partner Agreement v2.4

1. Partner Onboarding & Qualification

As an independent Driver, Machinery Operator, or Fleet Owner on Rentron, you confirm that you possess a valid commercial driver's license, machinery operation endorsements (where applicable), and mandatory regulatory permits.

2. Vehicle & Machinery Documentation

All onboarded equipment (JCB excavators, loaders, commercial cabs, and utility vehicles) must maintain up-to-date Registration Certificates (RC), active Insurance Policies, and Pollution/Fitness clearances. Rentron reserves the right to suspend partner accounts with expired documentation.

3. Job Acceptance & Progressive Dispatch

Job offers are dispatched based on proximity, vehicle category match, and partner online availability. Once a job offer is accepted, the partner agrees to mobilize to the designated site promptly. Repeatedly cancelling or abandoning accepted jobs may impact partner tier rating.

4. Earnings & Payout Settlements

Partner earnings are calculated according to agreed rate cards, distance, and metered equipment runtime. Settlements are credited directly to your registered bank account or UPI ID in accordance with regular payment cycles.

5. Site Safety & Professional Conduct

Operators must prioritize occupational safety at all job sites, utilize mandatory personal protective equipment (PPE), and adhere to local safety regulations. Substance abuse or reckless operation will result in immediate permanent deactivation.

Partner Support Desk: driver-support@rentron.in

Partner Privacy Standards v2.4

1. Telemetry & Real-Time GPS Tracking

When your driver status is set to ONLINE, the Rentron Driver app continuously transmits high-accuracy GPS coordinates to our dispatch engine to enable job matching, customer ETA updates, and emergency tracking.

2. Document & Identity Verification

We securely collect and store digital copies of your driver's license, Aadhaar/identity proof, bank account details, and vehicle RC for verification, KYC compliance, and direct payout processing.

3. Device Permissions

The driver app requires device permissions including Background Location, Push Notifications (for incoming job alerts), and Camera/Storage (for uploading documents and vehicle photos).

4. Data Confidentiality

Customer contact details provided during active trips are intended solely for trip coordination. Storing, distributing, or contacting customers after trip completion for personal purposes is strictly prohibited.

Privacy inquiries: privacy@rentron.in
